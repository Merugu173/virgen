About Me

From Dream to Reality

Straight out of my B.Tech in Metallurgical and Material Science from NIT Warangal, I dove headfirst into the entrepreneurial world I had dreamed about since graduation.

My journey began with Orgen, a B2C platform that taught me invaluable lessons through failure. Rather than stepping back, I pivoted into the technical space, immersing myself in futuristic technologies like AR, VR, AI, and Data Science.

Today, I'm channeling my expertise into sustainable manufacturing, transforming agricultural waste into energy solutions for a greener tomorrow.

My Journey

Healthcare & Digital Ventures
Expanded into healthcare with a physiotherapy clinic and launched a digital marketing agency focused on tier-2 cities with untapped potential. Co-founded a startup launchpad to help entrepreneurs from ideation to market entry.
2024 - Present
Tarunati Industries Pvt Ltd
After a year of research in waste management and agricultural residue, founded Tarunati Industries to manufacture pellets and briquettes from agro waste, supplying sustainable energy solutions to power plants. Leveraging my metallurgical background for environmental impact.
